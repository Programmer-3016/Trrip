Trrip Dining Experience Redesign Audit

find_in_page 1. Audit of Current Screens (Screenshots Analysis)

Observation 1: Truncation & Low Hierarchy in Discovery Cards

What we see: In the original app, restaurant titles ("Surya Mahal Resta...") and landmark proximity ("0.8 km from Albert Hall M...") are cut off with ellipses.

Why it matters: A traveller visiting Jaipur doesn't know the full place or landmark name. Truncation breaks decision confidence and creates cognitive friction.

Redesign Fix: Responsive horizontal split cards with full title wrapping, clear landmark tags ("Near Albert Hall"), and auto-rickshaw transit times ("4 min auto").

Observation 2: Ambiguous & Premature CTAs ("Pay Bill" & "Prebook")

What we see: On the current Restaurant Detail screen, the bottom sticky bar has equal-weighted "Prebook Now" and "Pay Bill" buttons.

Why it matters: A traveller researching dining before visiting doesn't need "Pay Bill" — that is an in-restaurant post-dining state! It confuses discovery with checkout.

Redesign Fix: Replaced with a unified primary CTA "Prebook Table (10% OFF)" + secondary "Get Directions / Call". Post-dining payment is contextualized only when physically checked in.

Observation 3: Missing Traveller Context (Signature Food & Heritage Vibe)

What we see: Original detail screen has zero food imagery and says: "Known local restaurant for Family Restaurant meals" (tautological & uninformative).

Why it matters: Travellers want destination-specific answers: "Is this authentic Rajasthani food? What is their famous dish? Is it pure veg?"

Redesign Fix: Added a dedicated "Must-Try Local Delicacies" section showcasing Dal Baati Churma Thali, Royal Kesari Thandai, dietary badges (Pure Veg, Jain food), and authentic food photography.

Observation 4: Clunky Filters & Visual Clutter

What we see: Original discovery uses heavy skeuomorphic 3D tabs, muddy brown palette, and huge pill buttons taking up 30% of screen height.

Why it matters: Travellers need quick, 1-tap filtering for immediate needs (e.g., Pure Veg, Rooftop sunset, Open Now).

Redesign Fix: Streamlined sticky chip carousel (All Spots, Pure Veg, Heritage Rooftop, Open Now, 10%+ Offers) and clean modern teal-accented design tokens.

format_list_numbered 2. Top 5 Prioritised Improvements (Impact Matrix)

  1. Interactive 2-Screen Flow: Discovery screen seamlessly connects to a rich Restaurant Detail screen and an instant table reservation modal.
  2. Traveller-First Decision Triggers: Added signature local dishes (Dal Baati Churma, Thandai), heritage vibe tags, and travel times from landmarks.
  3. Cleaner Action Hierarchy: Removed misleading "Pay Bill" button; elevated table booking with transparent Trrip discounts.
  4. Data Integrity & Ergonomics: Fixed duplicate address text and postal codes from original app; integrated direct Google Maps navigation.
  5. Visual Polish & Accessibility: Modernized typography (Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter), WCAG AA contrast, and smooth micro-interactions.
